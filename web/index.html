How it works

Three steps, fully verified end to end.

Every document is encrypted for one student and checked at every handoff — no plaintext templates, no unverified submissions.

01

Issue

A professor uploads a PDF template. DocuTrack encrypts a unique copy for each student with AES-256-GCM and signs an immutable header with RSA-PSS.

02

Receive

The student downloads a sealed package — an encrypted ZIP — and decrypts it locally. Any change to the package fails verification before it opens.

03

Submit & verify

Submitted work is checked against the signed header and SHA-256 hashes. Tampering and duplicate answers are flagged automatically.

Security

Built on standard, auditable cryptography.

No custom schemes — just well-understood primitives applied to confidentiality, authenticity, and integrity.

Confidentiality

AES-256-GCM

Authenticated encryption keeps every issued document confidential and cryptographically bound to its header.

Authenticity

RSA-PSS-SHA256

Each document header is digitally signed, so its origin and contents cannot be forged or silently altered.

Integrity

SHA-256

Content hashing detects tampered submissions and catches duplicate answers shared between students.
